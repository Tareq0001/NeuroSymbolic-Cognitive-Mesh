## 🏛️ Executive Architecture

**NeuroSymbolic Cognitive Mesh** combines the perceptual power of deep representations with the rigorous, zero-hallucination guarantees of **Formal Deductive Logic (Datalog & First-Order Predicates)**. Every conclusion generated by the system is backed by a formally verifiable proof tree.

### 🔬 Core Scientific Foundations
1. **Mathematical Hypergraphs ($G = (V, E)$):**
   Hyperedges $e \in E$ enclose arbitrary subsets of vertices $e \subseteq V$, modeling multi-party causal dependencies and spatio-temporal threats with barycenter force-directed clustering.
2. **First-Order Horn Clause Deductive Engine:**
   $$\text{Head}(X) \leftarrow \text{Body}_1(X, Y) \wedge \text{Body}_2(Y, Z) \wedge \dots$$
   Forward-chaining fixed-point solver with Most General Unification ($\text{MGU}$).
3. **Formal Verification Proof Trees:**
   Generates step-by-step resolution traces from axiomatic ground truths to the final guaranteed theorem ($\text{Q.E.D.}$).
